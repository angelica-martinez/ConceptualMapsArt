Movement 1: 17th & 18th Centuries

The Rococo Period: Aristocratic Extravagance

Popular during the 17th and 18th centuries, Rococo art celebrated the and leisure of the wealthy European ruling class.

1. Subject Matter

Playful, figures engaged in lighthearted, frivolous courtly romance and leisure.

2. Line & Form

Flowing lines, S curves, and ornate organic forms inspired by shells and nature.

3. Color Palette

Soft, airy colors such as blush pink, baby blue, and creamy gold.

4. Brushwork

Feathery, delicate brushstrokes that create a shimmering, dreamlike surface.

Jean Honoré Fragonard, The Swing (1767)

Imperial & Visual Language

The Byzantine Empire & Visual Characteristics

Carrying forward Roman imperial traditions, Byzantine artists rejected three dimensional realism to depict the eternal spiritual realm.

1. Flatness & 2D Space

Figures appear flat and weightless, reminding us visually of the flat figures in Ancient art.

2. Floating Figures

Without ground lines or linear perspective, sacred bodies appear to in heavenly space.

3. Halos

Luminous golden circles called frame the heads of holy figures to signify holiness.

4. Color & Gold

Rich, vibrant pigments paired with radiant backgrounds evoke divine light.

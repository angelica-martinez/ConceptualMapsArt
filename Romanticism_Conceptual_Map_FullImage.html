Romanticism in Action: Revolution, Justice & Sublime Nature

Revolution & Class Unity

Liberty Leading the People (1830)

Artist:

People from different social classes unite behind the allegorical figure of Liberty during the French July Revolution, capturing dynamic movement, smoke, and revolutionary passion.

Eugène Delacroix, Liberty Leading the People (1830)
Oil on canvas, Louvre: class struggle, equality, and dramatic revolutionary energy
Justice, Equality & Human Suffering

The Raft of the Medusa (1818 to 1819)

Artist:

Exposes government negligence and class injustice after a French naval shipwreck, using turbulent ocean waves, diagonal movement, and raw desperation to demand moral accountability.

Théodore Géricault, The Raft of the Medusa (1818 to 1819)
Oil on canvas, Louvre: heightened emotion, social justice critique, and dramatic turmoil
Individuality & Power of Nature

Wanderer above the Sea of Fog (c. 1818)

Artist:

A solitary individual stands atop a rocky peak gazing into vast, untamed mountains and mist, rejecting industrial factories to contemplate the sublime power and beauty of nature.

Caspar David Friedrich, Wanderer above the Sea of Fog (c. 1818)
Oil on canvas, Kunsthalle Hamburg: solitary individuality and awe before untamed nature
